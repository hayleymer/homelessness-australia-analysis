## Homelessness in Australia — trends, vulnerability and drivers
An analysis of specialist homelessness services data from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW), covering July 2017 to March 2026. This project examines the scale, vulnerability profile and drivers of need across Australia's homelessness support system.

### Interactive visualisation
[View on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/hayley.merat7756/viz/HomelessnessinAustraliatrendsvulnerabilityanddrivers/HomelessnessinAustralia)

### Key findings

Queensland's client numbers have nearly tripled since 2017 — the fastest growth of any state
Since mid-2022, clients who are already homeless have consistently outnumbered those at risk of homelessness — the gap now exceeds 5,000 per month
Financial stress is now the #1 driver of people seeking support, up 47% since 2017 — overtaking accommodation as the primary reason

### Data source
Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, Specialist Homelessness Services Collection, July 2017 – March 2026
